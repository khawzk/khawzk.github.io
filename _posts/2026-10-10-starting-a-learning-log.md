Most of what I learn at work disappears into chat threads and slide decks. This page is where I'll write it down instead: short notes on cloud architecture, GenAI models and the pre-sales craft of turning both into something a customer can sign off on.

## What goes here

- **Things I tried.** A model, an API, a deployment pattern, and what actually happened.
- **Ways of thinking.** How I scope a PoC, price a GenAI workload, or compare two architectures.
- **Notes from studying.** Certifications, papers and courses, in my own words.

## Rules I'm keeping

1. Short is fine. A note can be five sentences.
2. Public products and my own experiments only. No customer names and no internal numbers.
3. Some notes will be in English, some in 中文, and the important ones in both.
